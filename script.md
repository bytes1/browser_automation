# Cultural Browser Studio — Live Demo Script (`script.md`)

> **Target Duration:** 3 minutes (Full Demo) + 60-second Lightning Version included at the bottom  
> **Core Stack Highlighted:** Browserbase + Stagehand · Qloo Taste AI · Trigger.dev · Liveblocks · Wispr Flow · Google Gemini · Resend

---

## Pre-Demo Checklist (Before You Hit Record)

1. **Tab 1 — Landing Page (`/`)**: Open the 3D hero landing page so you can start with a strong visual hook.
2. **Tab 2 — Workflow Studio (`/workflows/<id>`)**: Have a workflow open with the **"Web-to-Taste Auto-Discovery"** blueprint loaded (or have a completed run in the bottom console so you can show instant outputs AND trigger a fresh live Browserbase run).
3. **Tab 3 — Multi-Branch Blueprint**: Either have **"Subculture & Competitor Positioning"** ready to click in the Right Sidebar under **Starter Blueprints**, or pre-loaded in a second workflow tab.
4. **Zoom Level**: Set browser zoom to `100%` (or `110%` on 1440p/4K displays) and click **Fit View** in the top-left canvas pill so all nodes are crisp and readable.

### 3. Live Browserbase + Stagehand Execution: "Web-to-Taste Auto-Discovery" (1:05 – 2:00)

**[SCREEN ACTION]**
- Show the **Web-to-Taste Auto-Discovery** graph on the canvas:
  `Start` → `Open Cultural Page` → `Extract Creators & Works` → `Auto-Resolve Qloo Seeds` → `Cross-Domain Brand Match` → `Synthesize Sponsorship Brief`.
- Click the purple **Run** button in the top-right header.
- Open the bottom **Console** drawer and switch to the **Cloud Browser (`LIVE` / `REPLAY`)** tab while `Open Cultural Page` and `Extract Creators & Works` are running so viewers see the live **Browserbase** Chromium session in action.
- Once steps complete, click through the steps in the Console (`Extract Creators & Works` → `Auto-Resolve Qloo Seeds` → `Cross-Domain Brand Match` → `Synthesize Sponsorship Brief`) to show the visual **Qloo Cards**, **Constellation View**, and **Gemini Executive Brief**.

**[SPEAK]**
> "Let’s run our first pipeline: **Web-to-Taste Auto-Discovery**.
>
> When I click **Run**, **Trigger.dev** orchestrates the workflow in the background and spins up a stealth cloud browser session on **Browserbase**.
>
> If we switch to the **Cloud Browser** tab in our console, we can watch our **Browserbase** session live right inside the IDE.
> - First, **Stagehand** navigates to a live cultural page — here, the Wikipedia page for *Studio Ghibli* — and autonomously extracts the top iconic works and creators from the DOM.
> - Next, that live web text is piped directly into **Qloo Taste Seeds**, which resolves the scraped text into canonical Qloo entity IDs.
> - Those entity IDs flow into **Qloo Cross-Domain Brand Match**, discovering high-affinity lifestyle brands aligned with `craft, minimalist, design` vibes.
> - Finally, **Gemini** synthesizes the live Browserbase web extraction and Qloo affinity scores into a ready-to-send **Brand Partnership Pitch Brief**, complete with interactive affinity cards and a taste constellation graph."

### 4. Multi-Branch Founder Use Case: "Subculture & Competitor Positioning" (2:00 – 2:40)

**[SCREEN ACTION]**

- Click the `Your Startup Website (Drop URL)` node (`https://linear.app`) to show the **Editor** inspector on the right, then click one of the Competitor `Open URL` / `Extract` nodes (`Raycast`, `Vercel`, `Supabase`).

**[SPEAK]**
> "Now let’s look at a powerhouse use case for startup founders and marketers: **Startup Subculture & Competitor Positioning**.
>
> A founder drops their own live website URL into the first node — here we have `linear.app`.
> 1. **Browserbase and Stagehand** scrape the founder’s live homepage to extract their hero headline, value proposition, and positioning claims.
> 2. The graph then fans out into **three cultural subcultures** powered by **Qloo**:
>    - *Design & Indie Craft* (seeded with A24, Wes Anderson, Teenage Engineering)
>    - *High-Growth Tech Operators* (seeded with Christopher Nolan, Wired, Apple)
>    - *Creator & Gaming Subculture* (seeded with Studio Ghibli, Nintendo, Discord)

> 4. All three branches converge into our **AI Cultural Synthesizer**, generating a side-by-side **Subculture Perception & Competitor Positioning Brief** with concrete landing-page copy the founder can ship immediately."
